## The Business Book 🧾 (desktop apk) 
***minimal, reliable, offline, and personalized***



## Deskription

A professional level desktop application (production ready) that is for storing/tracking business profit/expense/revenue, analyse the business performance through informative analytical graphs, save the expenses and profit chart sheet in the format of excel file for further uses like filing Tax and all.

Key Features: 

* storing, editing, deleting and tracking daily expense of a small business through an easy interface.
* defining custom types of expenses and income with the transaction.
* can see and downloading the monthly expense/income reports in excel file.
* Shows analytical graphs of expenses, profits and revenue distribution.
* preview the expense before exporting it to the local storage in the form of excel file.
* Sequre, Dedicated, Usefull.
